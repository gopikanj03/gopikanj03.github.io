About

"The ERP I work on was a capable product, but much like any other. The AI layer is what makes it worth choosing - and that layer is mine!"

I work as a Junior Generative AI Engineer at Raabyt. The product when I arrived was a solid on-premise ERP: everything you would expect, nothing you would not. My work has been to build intelligence on top of it.

That means a conversational agent that answers questions across the entire system from a single prompt, a report generator that takes a plain-language description and builds the report however complex the query underneath, lead scores that re-weigh themselves as customer responses land, and data migration handled with AI - the hardest piece, and the one nobody sees.

Alongside those I build and integrate ML models for classification, forecasting and anomaly detection, placed in the workflows where decisions actually get made. Taken together, they moved the product from a standard ERP to one where the AI is the reason to pick it.
